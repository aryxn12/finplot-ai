PRODUCT ROADMAP

From a helpful prototype to a secure money model.

These improvements are planned capabilities. This page explains their purpose and separates the frontend experience from the backend systems required to make them reliable. The cards are a roadmap display—not a claim that every capability is already implemented.

2CRITICAL ITEMS
7HIGH PRIORITY
9IMPROVEMENTS
01 · ACCESSCRITICAL

User authentication

Provide sign-up, sign-in, sign-out and session-expiry experiences.

Frontend: login & signup UIBackend: identity & sessions
Why it matters
Users need a verified identity before their personal financial information can be accessed.
02 · PRIVACYCRITICAL

Private financial profiles

Give every account its own income, fixed costs, emergency fund, preferences and goals.

Frontend: private profile screensBackend: per-user storage
Why it matters
Each user's financial profile must remain private and separate from everyone else's.
03 · DATA PROTECTIONCRITICAL

Database user isolation

Ensure every transaction, goal, import and profile record is scoped to its owner.

Frontend: account-aware statesBackend: authorization checks
Why it matters
Prevent users from viewing or changing another user's records, even if they alter an API request.
04 · IMPORT QUALITYHIGH

Better PDF extraction

Support different bank statement layouts, extraction previews and correction before saving.

Frontend: upload & review UIBackend: PDF parsing
Why it matters
Different banks format statements differently; accurate extraction reduces missing or misclassified transactions.
05 · RECURRING PAYMENTSHIGH

Subscription change alerts

Show alerts when a recurring payment amount changes and flag services the user may want to review.

Frontend: alerts & settingsBackend: recurring analysis
Why it matters
Users can notice price increases and review subscriptions they may no longer use. Usage cannot be confirmed from bank transactions alone.
06 · CONVERSATIONAL HELPHIGH

AI integration

Add a natural-language assistant for questions about budgets, spending patterns and goals.

Frontend: chat interfaceBackend: AI model & safe context
Why it matters
People can understand their money model using everyday language instead of navigating complex reports.
07 · GOAL PLANNINGHIGH

Goal date projections

Estimate monthly contributions and show whether a target date appears achievable under current assumptions.

Frontend: projection chartsBackend: projection calculations
Why it matters
Users can adjust target dates or contributions when a goal may be difficult to reach.
08 · SPENDING INSIGHTSHIGH

Monthly budget drift

Compare planned category budgets with actual spending and highlight meaningful differences.

Frontend: budget chartsBackend: monthly calculations
Why it matters
Users can spot categories where spending is moving away from their plan before month-end.
09 · PLATFORM SAFETYHIGH

Security hardening

Build secure data handling with encryption, server-side validation, audit logs and reliable backups.

Frontend: safe forms & privacy UIBackend: security controls
Why it matters
Financial data is sensitive. Security controls help protect it from accidental exposure, tampering and loss.
Frontend contributor note: You can build the screens, forms, charts, loading states, alerts and responsive layouts. Authentication enforcement, private database access, PDF parsing, recurring-payment analysis, AI API calls, calculations that must be trusted, encryption, audit logs and backups require backend implementation. Never place API secrets or bank credentials in browser code.
FinPilot AI is an educational budgeting and scenario-modelling prototype. It does not provide investment, tax, legal or credit advice and does not recommend specific securities. Projections are estimates based on entered data, not guarantees.
